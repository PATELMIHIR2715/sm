# 10-Year Macro Regimes & Sector Impact Taxonomy (2015 – 2025)

This document serves as the historical domain baseline for the RAG engine when analyzing how news events land under different macro market conditions.

---

## 1. Major Macro Regimes & Black Swan Events

| Event / Period | Regime Type | Primary Impacted Sectors | Market Sentiment Pattern |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Demonetization (Nov 2016)** | Policy Shock | Real Estate, NBFCs, Retail (-), Digital Payments (+) | High short-term volatility; liquidity freeze in cash-heavy sectors. |
| **GST Rollout (July 2017)** | Structural Reform | Organized Logistics, FMCG (+), Unorganized Retail (-) | Medium-term supply chain transition, shift from unorganized to organized. |
| **IL&FS Crisis (Sept 2018)** | Credit / NBFC Crisis | NBFCs, Real Estate, Housing Finance (-) | Tightening liquidity, spike in borrowing costs. |
| **COVID-19 Pandemic (Mar 2020)** | Global Black Swan | Aviation, Tourism, Real Estate (-); Pharma, IT, Chemicals (+) | Sharp market crash followed by massive V-shaped recovery driven by liquidity. |
| **Global Inflation & Rate Hike Cycle (2022–2023)** | Macro Monetary Cycle | High-valuation Tech/IT (-), Export-heavy (-); Banks (+) | Margin compression across consumer goods due to raw material input costs. |
| **Russia-Ukraine War & Crude Shock (Feb 2022)** | Geopolitical Shock | Oil Marketers, Paints, Aviation, Tyres (-); Metals, Defense, Fertilizers (+) | Crude oil spikes above $100/bbl; fertilizer and commodity price surges. |
| **PLI Schemes & Capex Boom (2021–2025)** | Industrial Policy | Defense, Railways, Electronics, Auto Components, Solar (+) | Multi-year order book expansion for capital goods & defense PSU companies. |

## 2. Sectoral Sensitivity Multipliers

- **Defense & Capital Goods:** High sensitivity to government tender announcements, PLI updates, and GeM order wins.
- **Paints & Tyres:** High inverse correlation to crude oil prices (raw material cost driver).
- **IT & Exports:** Sensitive to USD/INR exchange rate, US Fed rate commentary, and client IT spending trends.
- **Banking & Financials:** Sensitive to RBI MPC rate decisions, net interest margins (NIM), and NPA/asset quality trends.
- **Small-Cap Universe:** Highly sensitive to order win value relative to annual revenue (`Order Size / Revenue > 15%` is typically high-impact).
